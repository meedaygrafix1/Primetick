Under the glass

Fig. 1 — A watch, taken apart

Six layers,
one tick

Most watches follow the same plan under the glass. Move across the drawing to open this one up, then up or down to pick a layer.

  • CrystalThe clear cover over the dial. Mineral, acrylic or sapphire, it takes the knocks so the face doesn't.
  • BezelThe ring that holds the crystal. On some watches it turns, to time a dive or a meeting.
  • DialThe face: hour markers, hands and the name. The part everyone sees first.
  • CaseThe body. Lugs hold the strap; the crown on the side sets the time.
  • MovementThe engine. Quartz runs on a battery; an automatic winds itself as you move.
  • CasebackSealed underneath. Some are see-through, so you can watch the movement work.
